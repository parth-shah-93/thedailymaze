    How to play Trail
    
      
    Find the hidden trail to the flag in 6 tries. Stones mark its 5 forks, the spots where you must pick the right way.
    
      
      
      
      
    Hard mode: no orange tiles or fork marks.
    
    
    
    
    
      
    How to play Fog
    
      
    Find the hidden flag in the dark. Tiles show how far away it is:
    hot (1–2) · neutral (3–4) · cold (5+)
    
      
      
    Dead ends put out your 5 torches. Lose them all and the fog wins.
    
      
    
        
        
      
    
      
      
    Hard mode: no turning back in a corridor.
