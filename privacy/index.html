Privacy

Last updated October 13, 2026

The short version: no accounts, no ads, no cookies. Visits are counted anonymously, and your results stay on your own device.

What's saved on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves a few things in your browser's local storage:

  • Today's game, so you can pick up where you left off
  • Your stats and streaks
  • Your settings, like Hard mode
  • Whether you've already seen the How to play guide

This stays in your browser and is never sent anywhere. It isn't a cookie, and the site doesn't use cookies. Clearing this site's data in your browser erases it.

Counting visits

Visits are counted anonymously with GoatCounter, to see how many people play. It uses no cookies, doesn't keep your IP address and can't identify you. See GoatCounter's privacy policy.

What else is collected

Nothing. There are no accounts and no ads. Apart from the visit counter, the game's code, fonts and images all come from this site.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address to run and secure its service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Sharing your result

The share button copies your result to your clipboard, and only when you tap it. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you paste it yourself.

Emailing support

If you write to support@thedailymaze.com, your message is forwarded by the domain provider to the site's inbox. Your email address and message are used only to reply to you, and are never sold or shared.

Children

The game doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If what's collected ever changes, this page will be updated first.
