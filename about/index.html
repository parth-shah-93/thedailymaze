About

The Daily Maze is made by one person, as a fun hobby and a way to keep learning. If you enjoy solving mazes, I hope it becomes part of your day.

Every day there are two new puzzles, and everyone in the world gets the same ones:

  • Trail: find the hidden trail from the start to the flag in 6 tries.
  • Fog: find the flag in a dark maze, following the heat, before your torches run out.

New mazes arrive at midnight, your time. There's nothing to sign up for and nothing to install. Just play, then share your result.

Ideas, questions, or a maze that seemed broken?

Write to support@thedailymaze.com. Every message gets read.
